Privacy Policy & Legal Disclaimer

Last Updated: September 2026

1. Public Source & Manual Availability

The student directory information (names, roll numbers, sessions, and profile pictures) presented on this website is compiled exclusively from publicly accessible records on Rangpur Govt College's web portal (https://rgc.eshiksaems.com/). Any user with a standard browser can access and manually retrieve these exact same public records.

2. Non-Commercial & Educational Purpose

This service is an independent community directory provided solely for educational indexation, peer discovery, and student convenience under HSCStack. It does not sell student data, charge access fees, or display commercial third-party advertisements.

3. No Sensitive Personal Information (PII)

We do not collect, process, or store sensitive personal data such as phone numbers, personal email addresses, home addresses, dates of birth, National Identification Numbers (NID/Birth Certificates), or examination mark sheets.

4. Content Removal & Privacy Opt-Out

We respect individual privacy rights. If you are a registered student or legal guardian and prefer not to have your public directory listing indexed on this platform, you may request removal. Submit your Roll Number through our Support Center, and your listing will be permanently delisted within 24–48 hours.

5. Non-Affiliation Disclaimer

This platform is an independent student community initiative powered by HSCStack. It is not affiliated with, authorized, maintained, or endorsed by Rangpur Govt College or eShiksa EMS.
